## Slide 2: Executive Summary & Context
- **The Challenge:** Retail banking customer acquisition costs 5–7× more than retention. Unchecked churn leads to deposit flight and revenue vulnerability.
- **The Portfolio:** 10,000 customers analyzed across 3 core European geographies.
- **Key Headline:** 
  - **Overall Churn Rate:** **20.37%** (~1 in 5 accounts exited).
  - Churn is **highly clustered** in specific geographical, demographic, and financial cohorts.

---

## Slide 3: Geographic Exposure (The Germany Problem)
- **Data Points:**
  - **Germany:** 32.44% Churn Rate | **Risk Index: 1.59 (High Risk)**
  - **Spain:** 16.67% Churn Rate | Risk Index: 0.82
  - **France:** 16.15% Churn Rate | Risk Index: 0.79
- **Key Observation:** Germany contributes nearly 40% of all churned accounts despite representing only 25% of the portfolio.
- **Strategic Implication:** Local fintech disruption (e.g., N26) and fee dissatisfaction demand a dedicated German market turnaround strategy.

---

## Slide 4: Demographic Vulnerability (The 46–60 Age Bracket)
- **Age Cohort Breakdown:**
  - `<30 Years:` 7.55% Churn Rate
  - `30–45 Years:` 13.88% Churn Rate
  - `46–60 Years:` **43.29% Churn Rate (Peak Risk)**
  - `60+ Years:` 25.44% Churn Rate
- **Key Observation:** Churn spikes aggressively past age 45. Mature clients with high accumulated wealth are exiting at more than 3× the rate of younger clients.

---

## Slide 5: Capital Flight Risk (High-Value Customers)
- **Balance Segmentation:**
  - Zero Balance: 13.82% Churn
  - Low Balance: 23.47% Churn
  - **High Balance (>€119,800): 24.69% Churn**
- **Financial Profile Comparison:**
  - Average Balance of Churned Customers: **€91,108.54**
  - Average Balance of Retained Customers: **€72,745.30**
- **Takeaway:** Churners are wealthier than retained customers. This represents significant deposit drain and liquidity exposure.

---

## Slide 6: Engagement Disparity & Gender Insights
- **The Engagement Drop Indicator:**
  - Active Members: 14.27% Churn
  - Inactive Members: **26.85% Churn**
  - **Engagement Gap:** **12.58 percentage points**
- **Gender Disparity:**
  - Female Churn: **25.07%** vs. Male Churn: **16.46%**
- **Takeaway:** Lack of recent account activity is the #1 early-warning indicator. Female account holders display an 8.6 pp higher tendency to exit.

## Slide 7: Predictive Machine Learning Architecture
- **Model Comparison (Stratified 80/20 Split):**
  - **Logistic Regression (Baseline):** 70.85% Accuracy | 0.501 F1 | 0.771 ROC-AUC
  - **Random Forest (Champion):** **85.90% Accuracy** | **0.589 F1** | **0.854 ROC-AUC**
- **Top 5 Predictive Features:**
  1. Customer Age
  2. Number of Products
  3. Active Member Status
  4. Balance
  5. Geography (Germany)

## Slide 9: Strategic Recommendations & Action Plan
1. **Tiered Premier Advisory for 45–60 Cohort:** Deploy dedicated wealth advisors and higher-yield savings products for mature high-balance savers.
2. **Automated CRM Reactivation Engine:** Trigger automated email and app check-ins when an account shows 60+ days of dormancy.
3. **German Market Remediation:** Re-evaluate checking fee structures and introduce modern mobile banking perks to match German fintechs.
4. **Product Bundling:** Incentivize holding 2+ complementary products to build switching barriers.
